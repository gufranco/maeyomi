Cards left out

The last digit of a barcode is a check digit worked out from the other twelve, so a mistyped digit on the wikiwiki.jp page shows up. Where the numbers that page gives for the card point to one digit, the card is repaired and printed. No single repair fits the numbers of the cards below, so they are left out rather than guessed.
