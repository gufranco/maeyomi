Cards Epoch really sold

These are the barcodes printed on the cards Epoch released in Japan, typed in by collectors on the Barcode Battler wiki. The numbers on each card are read from the barcode by this program, not copied from the wiki.

Cards left out

The wiki has a typo in these: the last digit does not match the others, so the machine would refuse them. Guessing the right digit would mean printing a card nobody ever sold, so they are left out instead.
